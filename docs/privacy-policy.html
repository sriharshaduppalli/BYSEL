Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026 · Effective immediately

Summary: BYSEL is a paper-trading and market-education app. We collect only what is needed to run accounts, simulations, and AI features. We do not sell personal data.

1. Who we are

BYSEL Services (“BYSEL”, “we”, “us”) operates the BYSEL mobile application and related backend services for Indian stock-market education and simulated (paper) trading.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Username and email for password-based accounts
  • Phone number for OTP / Firebase phone authentication
  • Optional display name if you provide it
  • Session tokens used to keep you signed in

Usage and simulation data

  • Paper trades, holdings, watchlists, alerts, and portfolio settings
  • Private stock notes you save on a symbol
  • AI Assistant queries and responses (to operate and improve the feature)
  • App preferences such as theme and refresh settings

Technical data

  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging device token (to deliver price-alert notifications you create)
  • Firebase Analytics usage events (app opens / basic diagnostics). Advertising ID collection is disabled
  • IP address for security and abuse prevention

What we do not collect

  • Real brokerage credentials or live bank account details
  • Contacts, photos, files, or microphone/camera content
  • Precise GPS location
  • Advertising ID (the App disables Google advertising ID collection)

3. How we use data

  • Authenticate your account and keep sessions secure
  • Deliver paper trading, market data, alerts, and AI analysis
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests you send us

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Limited sharing occurs only for:

  • OTP / SMS providers and Firebase Authentication — to deliver login codes
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver price-alert notifications to this device
  • Firebase Analytics — basic app-usage diagnostics (not advertising; no Advertising ID)
  • Cloud hosting (for example our API host) — to run the product
  • AI providers (for example Groq / Gemini / our Indian Stock LLM stack) — when you use AI chat; queries are stock-related prompts processed to return answers. We remain responsible for this processing under Play User Data requirements (limited use; not sold as marketing data)
  • Market data sources (public market data providers and exchange feeds via our backend) — publicly available market data only
  • Legal obligation — if required under applicable Indian law

5. Storage and security

  • Account and portfolio data may be stored on our servers and locally on your device
  • Transport uses HTTPS; access is limited to authorized systems
  • You can delete your account from Settings; we aim to remove associated personal data within 30 days

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or request deletion of your account data
  • Opt out of optional product emails where offered
  • Contact us with privacy concerns at any time

7. Children’s privacy

BYSEL is intended for users aged 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Contact us if you believe a minor has registered and we will delete the account.

8. Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs or ad networks for targeted ads.

9. Financial disclaimer

BYSEL provides simulated trading and educational analysis only. It is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not execute live brokerage orders. AI outputs are informational and can be wrong. Do your own research and consult a qualified adviser before real investing.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
